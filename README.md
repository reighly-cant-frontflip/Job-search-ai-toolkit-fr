# 🤖 Job Search AI Toolkit — Turn Claude.ai into your job search assistant

A ready-to-use kit that turns Claude.ai into a personal job search assistant: tailored CVs, sober cover letters, application tracking in Notion, and semi-automated job hunting with Claude in Chrome.

**Setup time: ~45 minutes.** No technical skills required.

> **Language note:** the guide and skill files are written in **French** and target the **French job market** (Paris / Île-de-France in particular). The structure works anywhere, but the market insights and phrasing conventions are France-specific.

---

## Who it's for

- Job seekers actively applying in France
- International or multilingual candidates who need to adapt their applications to French conventions
- Anyone applying to more than ten roles who wants to stop redoing everything by hand

---

## What you get

| Component | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Project Instructions** | Claude knows your full profile in every new conversation |
| **`skill-cv.md`** | Tailor your CV to a job posting in five minutes, ATS-proof |
| **`skill-lm.md`** | Write a 250–320 word cover letter with no filler phrases |
| **Notion tracker** | Track applications without manual data entry |
| **Claude in Chrome** | Semi-automated job hunting across multiple platforms |
| **Market context** | What actually works platform by platform, from the field in 2026 |

---

## Quick start

1. **Read the guide** → [`guide/job-search-ai-setup.md`](guide/job-search-ai-setup.md)
2. **Create a Project** on [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) and paste in your Project Instructions
3. **Download both skills** from [`skills/`](skills/) and upload them into your Project
4. **Upload your CV** as a PDF into the Project
5. **Connect Notion** (Settings → Integrations) if you want the tracker

The guide opens with a profile questionnaire — answer it directly in Claude and it will generate your personalised Project Instructions for you.
